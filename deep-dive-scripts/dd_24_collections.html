1
What the Collections Module Does

The Collections module gives you a structured, automated process for managing past-due accounts. Instead of relying on someone to manually remember to follow up on overdue invoices, you define a collection strategy in templates, attach those templates to bill profiles, and let PSA identify which customers are past due and queue them for the appropriate next step.

Collections keeps a complete audit history of every step taken on every account — who did what, when, and what the customer's response was. This is critical for dispute resolution and for understanding which customers are chronic late payers.

→ Connects ToCollections connects to: Bill Profiles (collection templates are assigned to bill profiles, which are then assigned to customers), Billing (the AR aging drives which accounts enter the collections queue), Customer Billing tab (each customer has a Collections sub-tab showing their full collections history), and Workflows (future integration for automated collection-triggered communications).
▲ Platform ImpactWithout a collections process, overdue accounts are managed ad hoc — whoever remembers to call, calls. Accounts that aren't followed up on turn into write-offs. The Collections module turns collections from a reactive, inconsistent effort into a documented, systematic process.
2
Collection Templates — Designing Your Strategy

A Collection Template defines the sequence of steps taken when a customer becomes overdue. Navigate to Admin > System Settings > Billing Config > Collection Templates > New Template.

Template Fields

FieldDescription
Template NameDescriptive name: 'Standard Net 30 Collections,' 'Enterprise Escalation Process'
Balance LimitOptional minimum balance before an account enters the collections queue. Avoids chasing very small balances.
Collection StepsThe sequence of actions in order

Collection Step Types

  • Email: Send a templated email to the customer. Configure the subject, body, and which contact receives it.
  • SMS: Send a text message to the customer's mobile number.
  • Manual Task: Create a task for your billing team to complete — phone call, physical letter, account review. The step requires a human action before the queue advances.
  • Account Hold: Flag the customer account with a hold status.
  • Service Suspension: Trigger a service suspension action on the customer's account.

3
Assigning Templates to Bill Profiles

Once a collection template is built, assign it to a Bill Profile. From Admin > System Settings > Billing Config > Bill Profiles, edit the relevant profile and select the collection template in the Collections section.

Since customers are assigned to bill profiles, attaching a template to a bill profile automatically applies that collection strategy to every customer on that profile. This is why your bill profile structure matters — customers with different collection treatment should be on different profiles.

Bill ProfileCollection Template
Monthly Net 30 — StandardStandard Net 30 Collections (email reminders at 15, 30, 45 days)
Enterprise QuarterlyEnterprise Escalation (longer grace period, phone-first approach)
Residential Due on ReceiptResidential Collections (shorter timeline, suspension at 30 days)
Internal / TestNo template assigned
4
Running the Collections Queue

Navigate to Billing > Collections. The screen shows all active collection steps with a count of customers currently In Queue for each step.

Executing a Step

  • Find a collection step with customers in the In Queue column
  • Click the menu icon and select View
  • Review the list of customers queued for this step — verify the balance and days past due
  • Select the customers you want to process (individual or Select All)
  • Click Execute Step — automated steps (email, SMS) fire immediately; manual steps create a task
  • Alternatively, click Assign User to delegate the step to a specific team member

Queue Rules

A customer stays in the collections queue until their overdue balance is paid in full. If a customer makes a partial payment that brings them below the Balance Limit threshold, they may drop out of the queue — but full resolution requires full payment of all overdue invoices.

💡 Pro TipRun the collections queue on a fixed schedule — weekly or bi-weekly. Batch processing keeps your collections effort consistent and efficient rather than ad hoc.
5
Customer Collections History

Every action taken in the collections process is recorded on the customer's profile under the Billing tab > Collections sub-tab. This shows the full history: which steps were executed, when, by whom, and the outcome if it was a manual step.

This history is critical for three situations: a customer disputes the collections process ('you never contacted me'), a customer relationship escalates to legal action (you have documented evidence of every contact attempt), or a new billing admin takes over an account and needs to understand the history.

💡 Pro TipBefore writing off an account, export the Collections history as documentation of all contact attempts. Even if you don't pursue legal action, this record protects you in any future dispute with that customer.

Action Items

  • Define your collection strategy — steps, timing, and escalation path for each customer tier
  • Build a collection template for each distinct strategy
  • Assign collection templates to the appropriate bill profiles
  • Establish a weekly or bi-weekly collections queue review as a standing process
  • Assign a specific team member as the collections queue owner
  • Review the AR Aging report before each collections run to identify new entrants
