1
The Integrations Library

Rev.io PSA's Integrations Library is the central hub for connecting PSA to third-party systems. Navigate to Admin > Integrations Library. The Marketplace tab shows all available integrations. The Instances tab shows integrations you've already configured. The Executions and Logs tabs show sync history and any errors.

Integrations are managed through Prismatic, a middleware platform that handles the authentication and data mapping between PSA and external systems. You don't need to configure Prismatic directly — the integration wizard inside PSA guides you through the setup.

▲ Platform ImpactEach integration you configure eliminates a class of manual data entry or manual data reconciliation. A QuickBooks integration eliminates manual invoice export. An RMM integration eliminates manual asset import. A distributor integration eliminates manual cost lookup. The cumulative time saved compounds significantly as integrations stack.
2
Accounting Integrations

QuickBooks Online

The QuickBooks Online integration synchronizes customer, invoice, and payment data between Rev.io PSA and QuickBooks Online. Invoices posted in PSA sync to QuickBooks, and payments recorded in either system stay in sync.

Configuration: Admin > Integrations Library > QuickBooks Online > Configure. You'll authenticate with your QuickBooks credentials, map PSA customer accounts to QuickBooks customers, and map PSA catalog items to QuickBooks products/services.

❓ PromptDo you use QuickBooks Online as your accounting system? If yes, let's set up the integration so your billing data flows automatically.
⚠ Watch OutQuickBooks Online integration requires careful initial data mapping. If customers or products don't match between systems, sync errors will occur. Plan time for a data review before activating the sync.

Google & Outlook Calendar Integrations

Sync your Rev.io PSA calendar with Google Calendar or Microsoft Outlook. When you schedule an event in PSA, it appears in your connected calendar and vice versa. During setup, you'll be prompted to select a default customer for any calendar item that doesn't have an associated customer.

Configuration path: Admin > Integrations Library > Google Integration or Outlook Integration > Admin Configuration, then each user completes the User Configuration to connect their personal calendar.

3
RMM Integrations

RMM (Remote Monitoring and Management) integrations connect PSA to the platform your team uses to monitor and manage customer endpoints. The integrations synchronize customer and asset data between systems, so devices managed in the RMM automatically appear as assets in PSA.

NinjaOne (Newest)

Bidirectional synchronization between NinjaOne organizations and Rev.io customers. Changes in either system are reflected in the other in real time.

N-Able N-Central

One-way customer sync from Rev.io to N-Central. When customers are pushed from Rev.io, matching organizations are automatically created in N-Central and linked back to Rev.io.

Acronis

Initially syncs customers from Rev.io to Acronis. The integration now also automatically creates tickets in PSA when active Acronis alerts fire, and closes those tickets when the alert is dismissed in Acronis. Initial sync imports all active alerts from the last 7 days.

❓ PromptWhich RMM platform does your team use? Let's check whether there's a native integration available and walk through the setup.
→ Connects ToRMM integrations connect to: Customer accounts (organizations sync both ways), Assets (managed devices appear with a 'Managed by [provider]' indicator and a Device Details tab with RMM-sourced data), and Tickets (Acronis auto-creates and auto-closes tickets based on alert state).
4
Distributor Integrations — Auto Cost Sync

Two major distributor integrations are now available that automatically sync your negotiated product costs from your distributor account directly to your PSA catalog. This eliminates manual cost updates when distributor pricing changes.

TD Synnex

Syncs product costs daily to the Purchasing tab in your catalog, using your negotiated dealer pricing from your TD Synnex EC Express account. Updates existing products that already list TD Synnex as a vendor, or adds TD Synnex as a new vendor based on manufacturer part numbers.

Setup requirements: Active TD Synnex EC Express account, whitelist Prismatic's outbound IPs with TD Synnex, then enter your credentials in the Configuration Wizard.

Ingram Micro

Syncs product costs from the Ingram Micro Xvantage Reseller API daily, reflecting your negotiated dealer costs. Setup requires an active Ingram Micro reseller account and registered API credentials.

❓ PromptDo you source products from TD Synnex or Ingram Micro? If so, setting up the cost sync means your catalog always reflects your actual current costs — critical for accurate margin reporting.
💡 Pro TipEven if you don't use the distributor integrations for automatic ordering, the cost sync alone is valuable. Knowing your current dealer cost on every catalog item makes your profitability reports reliable instead of stale.
5
CRM and Other Integrations

HubSpot

One-way sync of customer and contact data from HubSpot to Rev.io PSA. When a deal closes in HubSpot and a customer is created, that customer and their contacts sync automatically to PSA — ready for service delivery without manual re-entry.

Pax8

Connect Rev.io PSA to your Pax8 reseller account for product and billing synchronization.

Hudu

Integration with Hudu, the documentation and password management platform used by MSPs. Syncs customer data between the systems.

COPS

One-way customer sync from Rev.io to COPS (central station monitoring platform), with bidirectional contact sync. Changes sync in real time via webhooks.

Slack & Microsoft Teams

Connect PSA workflows to Slack channels or Teams channels. Workflow notifications can be posted directly to your team's chat channels instead of (or in addition to) email and SMS.

❓ PromptWhich of these systems do you use today? Let's prioritize which integrations to set up based on where your team spends the most time switching between systems.
6
Commerce Hub

Commerce Hub is a separate module accessible from the Admin sidebar. It's a marketplace where you can browse Rev.io's technology partner ecosystem, see available products and services from partners, and initiate contact with partners you're interested in.

It's not a transactional platform — you can't purchase directly through it — but it serves as a single place to discover integrations, tools, and services that work with Rev.io PSA. Think of it as a curated partner directory specific to the PSA ecosystem.

Action Items

  • Inventory all third-party systems currently used by the business
  • Check the Integrations Library marketplace for each system
  • Prioritize integrations by time-savings impact: accounting first, then RMM, then distributor
  • Gather API credentials and admin access for each system before scheduling integration setup
  • Configure QuickBooks Online if applicable — plan for initial data mapping time
  • Set up Slack or Teams notification channels if your team uses either platform
  • Connect Google or Outlook calendar for any users who want schedule sync
