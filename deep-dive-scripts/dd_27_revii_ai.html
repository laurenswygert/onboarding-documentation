The Rev.io PSA AI Layer — Three Distinct Tools

Rev.io PSA has three AI-powered tools that work together but serve different purposes. Understanding the distinction helps you use each one appropriately.

ToolWhat It DoesWhere It LivesBest Used For
Revii (Ask Revii)A conversational AI agent that can take actions in PSA, retrieve information, manage records, and answer operational questions in natural languagePersistent button at the bottom of every screenManaging PSA without navigating — 'create a ticket for Acme Corp,' 'what's my open ticket count today'
Revii Data AnalyticsA data analysis mode within Revii that queries your operational data across all modules and returns charts, tables, and summariesClick Ask Revii → select Revii Data Analytics modeAnswering business questions without building a report — 'show me revenue by customer for Q3,' 'which tech closed the most tickets last month'
ReviizeAn inline text improvement tool that appears in text fields throughout PSA — helps refine, clarify, and professionalize text you've writtenInside text fields on ticket Work Requested, time logs, activity notes, and opportunitiesImproving the quality of written communication before sending or saving
→ Connects ToRevii connects to every module in PSA — it can access customers, tickets, assets, inventory, billing, calendar, time logs, and contracts. Revii Data Analytics connects to the Analytics module's data layer. Reviize is available in Tickets, Time Logs, Activity Notes, and Opportunities.
2
Ask Revii — The Conversational Agent

Open Revii by clicking the Revii button at the bottom of any screen. The panel opens in the lower-right corner and is resizable — you can continue working in PSA while Revii is open. Your previous conversations are saved and can be renamed for reference.

What Revii Can Do

  • Retrieve information: 'Show me all open tickets for Acme Corp,' 'What is John Smith's current time logged this week?'
  • Create records: 'Create a new ticket for Acme Corp, subject: Internet outage, priority: Critical'
  • Update records: 'Change the status of ticket 4521 to In Progress,' 'Assign ticket 4521 to Sarah Johnson'
  • Manage time: 'Clock me in,' 'Clock me out with a note: finished on-site visit'
  • Answer billing questions: 'What is Acme Corp's current outstanding balance?' 'Is Acme Corp enrolled in autopay?'
  • Manage inventory: 'How many Cisco RV340 routers do we have in stock?' 'Create a purchase order for 5 units from TD Synnex'
  • Summarize tickets: 'Summarize ticket 4521,' 'Summarize the parts and labor on ticket 4521'
  • Handle calendar: 'What's on my schedule for tomorrow?' 'Create a calendar event for Friday at 10 AM for Acme Corp'
❓ PromptLet's try Revii live. Tell me the two or three things you find yourself doing most often in PSA — the most repetitive lookups or status checks. Let's see if Revii can handle them.
💡 Pro TipRevii works best with specific, concrete prompts. 'What open tickets do we have for Acme Corp with Priority High or Critical, created in the last 7 days?' will return better results than 'show me urgent tickets.'
3
Revii Voice — Hands-Free Operation

Revii supports voice interaction in two modes:

  • Voice Duplex: Real-time two-way voice conversation with Revii. Click the microphone icon in the Revii panel. Speak naturally — Revii listens, responds via audio, and can take actions. Seven voice options are available with adjustable speech speed.
  • Mobile Voice Commands: In the Rev.io Mobile app, technicians can use voice commands hands-free — useful when their hands are on equipment. The mobile Revii also provides real-time spoken feedback.

Voice Duplex is most valuable for: managers who want to check status while multitasking, dispatchers who are on the phone and need quick lookups, and techs in the field via mobile who can't type while working.

❓ PromptWould your team benefit from voice access to PSA data? Your dispatchers or managers might find it especially useful during busy periods when they're already on calls.
4
Revii Data Analytics — Business Intelligence by Conversation

Revii Data Analytics is accessed from within the Revii panel: click 'Ask Revii' and select the Revii Data Analytics option. This mode is specifically designed for data analysis — it queries your operational data across all modules and returns answers in chart, table, or summary format.

Example Queries

QuestionWhat Revii Returns
Which technician closed the most tickets last month?Ranked list of techs with ticket close counts
Show me revenue by customer for Q3Bar chart of revenue per customer account
What are my 10 largest outstanding invoices?Table of invoices sorted by amount with customer and due date
How many hours did we log on-site vs. remote this quarter?Comparison of time by labor type
Which customers haven't had a ticket in the last 90 days?List of dormant customer accounts
What is our average ticket resolution time by priority?Table of resolution times segmented by priority level

Revii Data Analytics is designed for confident, reliable analysis even on complex questions. It provides improved interpretation, faster insight generation, and surfaces outliers and patterns that would take significant manual report-building to find.

💡 Pro TipUse Revii Data Analytics for ad hoc business questions you have in the moment — the ones that would normally require asking someone in finance to pull a report. Over time you'll develop a library of go-to queries that you run regularly.
5
Reviize — Text Quality at the Point of Entry

Reviize is the AI text improvement tool embedded directly in text fields throughout PSA. Look for the Reviize button next to any supported text field. Click it after you've written your text, and Reviize will suggest an improved, more professional, clearer version.

Where Reviize Is Available

  • Ticket Work Requested field — improve client-reported issue descriptions before saving
  • Time Log Work Performed field — make time log descriptions clearer and more professional (important for billing transparency)
  • Ticket Activity Notes — improve notes before posting, especially customer-facing public notes
  • Opportunity activity notes — improve sales communications before posting
❓ PromptLet's try Reviize on a time log. Type a rough description of work performed — something a tech might type quickly — and then click Reviize. See how it transforms a vague entry into a clear, professional description.
▲ Platform ImpactTime log descriptions that go through Reviize before being saved become billable line items that read clearly on customer invoices. A tech entry of 'fixed router' becomes 'Resolved intermittent connectivity issue caused by misconfigured DHCP lease settings on the primary router.' That level of clarity reduces billing disputes significantly.

Action Items

  • Have every team member open Revii and try at least 5 natural language queries
  • Try Revii Data Analytics with your top 3 business questions
  • Enable Voice Duplex and test it with a dispatching or status-check scenario
  • Have field techs try Revii voice commands on the mobile app
  • Set up a team habit: use Reviize on every public activity note and time log before saving
  • Rename past Revii conversations that contain useful queries for future reference
