Pre-Session Checklist

  • Download the Rev.io Mobile app from the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android)
  • Have a test user credential available — ideally a technician-role user
  • Have at least one open ticket, one calendar event, and one customer with assets to demo
  • Review the Mobile Options category in Admin > System Settings before the session
1
The Mobile App — A Full Field Platform

The Rev.io Mobile app is not a simplified companion to the web platform — it is a full field operations platform for technicians. It covers the complete ticket lifecycle, customer management, scheduling, time tracking, inventory, billing, and AI assistance, all from a phone or tablet. Everything a tech needs to do their job is available without touching the web platform.

The app is available for iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play). Updates are released regularly — note that App Store and Google Play approval processes mean mobile updates may appear slightly after the corresponding web release.

→ Connects ToThe mobile app is a full PSA client: it reads from and writes to the same data as the web platform in real time. Status updates, time logs, notes, parts added, and signatures captured in the field sync instantly to the web platform — dispatchers and managers see them immediately.
2
Tickets in the Mobile App

The Tickets screen is the tech's primary workspace. They can view their assigned tickets, all open tickets (with permissions), and filter by user group.

What Techs Can Do on Tickets from Mobile

  • View all ticket details including customer info, description, priority, and status
  • Update ticket status in real time — Traveling, On-Site, Work Complete, Closed
  • Add public and internal activity notes with rich text, photos, and file attachments
  • Send and receive emails directly from the ticket Activity tab
  • Log time using the built-in timer or manual entry
  • Add Parts & Labor items from the catalog with inventory reserve capability
  • Capture customer signature on job completion
  • Create and complete tasks
  • View and manage linked tickets
  • Add, view, and link assets under Items Serviced
  • Create, view, and manage purchase orders
  • Apply ticket macros for quick standardized updates
  • View the ticket's AI-powered summary
  • Send ticket to billing directly from mobile
  • Generate an invoice directly from the ticket (with permission)
  • Delete non-email activity notes

Recently Viewed

A Recently Viewed overlay gives techs quick access to the last customers and tickets they opened — no searching needed to get back to what you were just working on.

3
Customers in the Mobile App

Techs can look up any customer from the Customers screen. The customer list displays the account number, and searching is available. From a customer detail screen, techs can access:

  • Customer summary and custom fields
  • All open and historical tickets for that customer
  • Assets at that customer's location
  • Calendar events and scheduled visits
  • Customer notes (with pop-up alert support for high-priority notes)
  • Contacts with phone tap-to-call and address tap-to-map
  • Unposted billing transactions (swipe to invoice or reverse)
  • Contracts list with full contract details
  • Payment history
  • Opportunities (create, edit, delete with activity notes)

Customers can be edited from mobile — swipe left on a customer in the list for the full edit screen, or tap Edit from the customer overview. Contacts can also be added and edited. Customer tags can be managed. Customer notes can be viewed, created, edited, and deleted.

GPS, Geofencing, and Location Features

Rev.io Mobile includes GPS tracking and geofencing features that must be configured in Admin > System Settings > Mobile Options before they activate.

FeatureWhat It DoesConfiguration
GPS TrackingTracks technician location while the app is activeEnable GPS Tracking in Mobile Options per user or globally
GeofencingAutomatically detects when a tech enters or exits a customer's locationRequires GPS coordinates on the customer's address record
Auto Status TransitionAutomatically changes ticket status when tech enters a geofenceConfigure in Mobile Options — e.g., auto-set to 'On-Site' when tech arrives
Auto Stop Travel TimerStops the travel timer automatically when tech enters the geofenceConfigure in Mobile Options
Customer SMS on ArrivalSends the customer an SMS when tech starts traveling, with ETARequires SMS to be enabled; ETA based on GPS distance
Geofence Activity LogCreates an activity entry on the ticket when tech enters/exits the geofenceAutomatic when geofencing is enabled
❓ PromptDo you want to track your field technicians' locations while they're on jobs? And would automatic status updates — the ticket moving to 'On-Site' when the tech arrives — help your dispatching visibility?
⚠ Watch OutGPS tracking requires technician consent and may have legal requirements depending on your jurisdiction. Consult your legal team before enabling GPS tracking for employees, especially in regions with strict employee monitoring laws.
5
Time Tracking from Mobile
  • Timer on tickets: Start and stop the built-in timer from any ticket — time is logged automatically with the labor item and reason.
  • Clock in/out from anywhere: Techs can clock in and out from anywhere in the app — not just from a specific ticket. A running timer displays at the top of the screen while clocked in.
  • Time Entry List: View all time entries in a card-based list with full details. Filter by All, Ticket, and Admin entries. Edit or delete entries based on permissions.
  • Visit tracking reminder: When a tech exits a tracked visit (via geofence) without creating a time log, the app reminds them with options to log now, dismiss, or snooze.
  • Admin time entries: Time entries not tied to a ticket (meetings, training, admin tasks) can be created directly from the app.
6
Scheduling and Calendar from Mobile
  • Calendar view: See the day's scheduled events and tasks. Tap any event to see full details.
  • Tasks: View, complete, and edit tasks. Multiple techs can be assigned to a single task — each sees it on their own calendar.
  • Offline calendar: Today's tasks are cached for offline access — viewable and editable without connectivity. Changes sync when connection is restored.
  • Push notifications: Receive push notifications for ticket assignments, status changes, and other events. Tap to navigate directly to the relevant ticket.
7
Inventory and Products from Mobile
  • Product catalog: Browse, search, and view the full product catalog. Sort and filter available.
  • Reserve inventory: Reserve inventory-managed products directly from a ticket. An indicator shows when inventory has been reserved.
  • Serialized inventory: Icons on parts and labor items indicate inventory-managed and serialized items.
  • Offline product browsing: Product catalog is cached for offline access. Inventory quantities disable when offline.
  • Manage assets: Add and edit customer assets from mobile including service dates, provider information, device specs, and asset status. View and edit asset custom fields.
8
Offline Mode

Rev.io Mobile supports offline operation for field technicians who work in areas with poor connectivity. When offline, the app serves cached data and queues changes for sync when connectivity is restored.

FeatureOffline Available?
View and update ticketsYes — status, notes, time entries, custom fields, signatures
Browse product catalogYes — using cached data (inventory quantities disabled)
View customer detailsYes — for customers visited today or associated with today's tasks
View and edit tasksYes — today's tasks cached
Sync changesAutomatic when connectivity is restored
⚠ Watch OutOffline mode syncs when connectivity is restored, but if multiple people update the same record while offline, conflicts may arise. Establish team norms about which tech is responsible for updating a shared ticket to minimize conflict risk.

Action Items

  • Have all field technicians download the app before go-live
  • Set up GPS tracking and geofencing in Mobile Options if desired — get legal sign-off first
  • Configure default time log reasons in Mobile Options for auto-populated travel logs
  • Test the full ticket workflow from mobile: assign → travel → arrive → log time → add parts → signature → close
  • Enable push notifications on each tech's device
  • Test offline mode by opening the app and then enabling airplane mode
  • Train techs on the clock-in/out feature for non-ticket time tracking
