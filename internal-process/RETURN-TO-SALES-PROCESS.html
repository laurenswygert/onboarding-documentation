REV.IO: RETURN TO SALES PROCESS

TRIGGERS

On-Hold Triggers

  • Client requests to delay the start of a project (updated timeframe will be documented in the initial contract)
  • Client states they cannot provide required materials to onboard (License fees still apply) but provides a timeframe to do so — Timeline is extended two weeks or more past the project plan milestone deliverable date on the project plan.
  • Client states that they do not have time to work on the project (License fees still apply) but provides a timeframe in which they can resume — Timeline provided is extended two weeks or more past the project plan milestone deliverable date on the project plan.

RTS Triggers

  • Project is on hold and the client does not pay license fees.
  • Overdue balance that has not been resolved. OC has reminded client at least twice.
  • Project is On-Hold past the date agreed upon with the client when they were originally placed on hold, with not clear start date within two weeks of the agreed upon date that the project would restart.
  • Client does not provide required deliverables within two weeks of the due date.
  • The client does not participate, fails to show for two weeks’ worth of calls, and/or fails to deliver any deliverables for more than two weeks.
  • Client refuses to participate in executive alignment calls
  • Client does not provide requests for additional information or updated deliverables within 2 weeks of the request after agreeing to do so
  • Client refuses to move forward due to an unforeseen requirement that we can not or will not deliver
  • Client states that they were promised or told something during the sale that we can't or won't do
  • Client is extremely unprofessional and disrespectful.
  • Note: Sales involvement will not expedite the delivery of required information or expedite client’s participation in the project

PROCESSES

On-Hold Process

  1. Project is placed “On-Hold”
  2. Client is notified that the project is On-Hold and all Rev.io involvement in project activity ceases
  3. An On-Hold review date is set if possible, based on when the Client tells us they can provide requirements or resume participation — Client must have a resume date to be on hold, otherwise, RTS.
  4. Sales is notified that the project is On-Hold and the reason(s) why
  5. OC sets expectations around estimated lead-time to resume a Project after client is ready to resume, and the new milestone dates for the project based on client providing missing deliverables
  6. OC — Project status is updated in Notion
  7. Projects are allowed 1 extension and then would be RTS

RTS Process

  1. The Onboarding Consultant will post in the project channel that they will be RTSing.
  2. They will @mention the sales rep as well as onboarding leadership.
  3. They will list a detailed explanation as to why they are returning the project to sales, including any outstanding deliverables, functionality misalignments, and any disputes that the client may have.
  4. Once that post is made, the OC will email the client using the templates that are also on the page, filling in the placeholders where necessary.
  5. Once that email has been sent, they will update the status in the Notion database to RTS and whatever the general category of RTS is.
  6. They will also fill in the RTS start date on the database so that that is there and list any of the reasons for RTS in the blockers field.
  7. Any calls that are scheduled will be removed from the calendar.
  8. Tickets should be placed into an on-hold status.

Note: This process includes all deliverables including but not limited to Project Charters being signed.
