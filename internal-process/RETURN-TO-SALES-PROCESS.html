REV.IO: RETURNED TO SOURCE PROCESS

GLOSSARY

  • RTS – Return to Source
  • OC – Onboarding Consultant
  • DE – Data Engineer
  • PS – Professional Services
  • SME – Subject Matter Expert
  • ON Leadership – Onboarding Leadership Team
  • AM Leadership – Account Management Leadership

TRIGGERS

On-Hold Triggers

  • Client requests to delay the start of a project (updated timeframe will be documented in the initial contract)
  • Client states they cannot provide required materials to onboard (License fees still apply) but provides a timeframe to do so — Timeline is extended two weeks or more past the project plan milestone deliverable date on the project plan.
  • Client states that they do not have time to work on the project (License fees still apply) but provides a timeframe in which they can resume — Timeline provided is extended two weeks or more past the project plan milestone deliverable date on the project plan.

RTS STATUS TYPES

RTS = Returned to Source

RTS Paperwork Incomplete

Definition: Used prior to kickoff, when a project has not yet been assigned to an Onboarding Consultant. Applied when something is missing from the sales side before onboarding can start — for example, an unsigned addendum, missing contract paperwork, or an outstanding PS sign-off.

Triggers:

  • Missing addendum(s)
  • Missing contract paperwork
  • Missing PS sign-off
  • Any other required sales-side documentation not yet received prior to onboarding starting

Example: A signed addendum covering a custom integration was never returned by the client, so the project cannot be assigned or scheduled.

Timeline: The project does not kick off while it is in this status. Once the missing paperwork is received, the project comes out of RTS and onboarding begins.

Process: See RTS Process – Paperwork Incomplete below for the full steps and templates.

RTS Client

Definition: Used when:

  • The client is unable to make deliverables on time
  • We have not received the data load by two weeks from kickoff
  • The client requests it due to a contract misalignment or payment terms misalignment
  • The client needs to stop the project for some reason but is unable to give us a return date
  • The client has been on hold for an extended amount of time and has gone through their allowed extensions

Triggers:

  • Project is on hold and the client does not pay license fees.
  • Overdue balance that has not been resolved. OC has reminded client at least twice.
  • Project is On-Hold past the date agreed upon with the client when they were originally placed on hold, with not clear start date within two weeks of the agreed upon date that the project would restart.
  • Client does not provide required deliverables within two weeks of the due date.
  • Client refuses to participate in executive alignment calls
  • Client does not provide requests for additional information or updated deliverables within 2 weeks of the request after agreeing to do so
  • Client is extremely unprofessional and disrespectful.

Example: The client’s main point of contact leaves the company mid-project and no replacement has been named, so the client asks to pause the project but cannot commit to a return date.

Timeline: Governed by the triggers above — for example, an On-Hold timeline extended two weeks or more past the agreed resume date, missed deliverables/calls for two weeks or more, or exhausting the one allowed On-Hold extension.

Process: See RTS Process – Client below for the full steps and templates.

RTS Functionality

Definition: Used when there is a product or functionality misalignment — the client is missing functionality they expected, or there is a mismatch between what was sold and what the product delivers. Whoever moves a project into this status must provide a detailed write-up covering: what the functionality gap is, a summary of the calls where it was discussed, and which Product Owner needs to be involved.

Triggers:

  • Client refuses to move forward due to an unforeseen requirement that we can not or will not deliver
  • Client states that they were promised or told something during the sale that we can't or won't do

Example: The client expected a specific reporting export that isn’t currently supported; the OC documents the gap, the calls where it came up, and identifies the Product Owner for that area.

Timeline: Resolution timeline depends on Product’s review of the write-up. The OC and the assigned Product Owner determine next steps and communicate a target timeline back to Onboarding leadership and Sales.

Process: See RTS Process – Functionality below for the full steps, including the Product confirmation call and internal write-up.

RTS Ghosting

Definition: Used when the client has not shown up for or responded to any communication for more than two weeks.

Triggers:

  • The client does not participate, fails to show for two weeks’ worth of calls, and/or fails to deliver any deliverables for more than two weeks.

Example: The client misses two consecutive project calls and does not respond to follow-up emails or calls from the OC for over two weeks.

Timeline: Moved to RTS Ghosting once two weeks of unresponsiveness has passed. The project stays in this status until the client becomes responsive again.

Process: See RTS Process – Ghosting below. The channel post must include the full outreach log — last contact date and all attempts made to reach the client.

Note: Sales involvement will not expedite the delivery of required information or expedite client’s participation in the project.

PROCESSES

On-Hold Process

  1. Project is placed “On-Hold”
  2. Client is notified that the project is On-Hold and all Rev.io involvement in project activity ceases
  3. An On-Hold review date is set if possible, based on when the Client tells us they can provide requirements or resume participation — Client must have a resume date to be on hold, otherwise, RTS.
  4. Sales is notified that the project is On-Hold and the reason(s) why
  5. OC sets expectations around estimated lead-time to resume a Project after client is ready to resume, and the new milestone dates for the project based on client providing missing deliverables
  6. OC — Project status is updated in Notion
  7. Projects are allowed 1 extension and then would be RTS
  8. About a week before the review date, the OC checks in with the client using the email below. Use the version that matches the RTS status the project would move to if the hold is not resolved.

Check-In Email – Leads to RTS Paperwork Incomplete

Uncommon – Paperwork Incomplete normally applies pre-kickoff. Use this version only if an already-active project is paused pending new paperwork, such as a contract amendment for expanded scope.

As discussed, we are going to move your project to an On Hold status until MM/DD/YY.

On-Hold means that all project work is suspended until MM/DD/YY. This includes all project and configuration calls and importer development work. You will still have access to ZenDesk to submit support tickets.

We will check in with you about a week before MM/DD/YY. If the outstanding paperwork has not been received by then, we will need to put your project in a “returned to source” status until it is resolved. This means your sales rep or account manager, REP NAME, will become your main point of contact with Rev.io during this time.

All project work — including calls, data loads, and configuration — will stop while your project is in RTS status and will not resume until you are back in onboarding.

Please note that your license fee is not affected by this change. If billing has already started, it will continue on its current schedule. If it has not started yet, it will begin on MM/DD/YY as outlined in your contract, regardless of project or RTS status.

RTS Process

Steps and messaging vary by RTS status. Use the walkthrough below that matches the status you are placing the project into. Anyone tagged in a channel post or internal notification is expected to acknowledge within 1 business day.

RTS Process – Paperwork Incomplete

  1. The OC (or Sales, since the project has not yet been assigned to onboarding) updates the project status in Notion to RTS – Paperwork Incomplete. The RTS start date is captured automatically; list the missing item(s) in the blockers field.
  2. Post in the project channel, tagging @ON Leadership, @Sales Leadership or @AM Leadership (whichever matches where the account originated), and the direct rep (Sales Rep or Account Manager):
RTS Status: Paperwork Incomplete
Client: CLIENT NAME

What the problem is: Describe the missing item – addendum, contract paperwork, PS sign-off, etc.

How to fix it: What needs to be signed or provided, and by whom, before the project can be assigned and kicked off

Contacts:
Client contact who needs to provide/sign – Name – Email – Phone
Rev.io contract owner – Name – Email

Relevant call recordings: N/A unless a discovery/sales call touched on this – link and timestamp if so

No client-facing notification for this status — Sales/Account Management handles the paperwork directly with the client. Since the project has not started, there are also typically no calls to remove from the calendar or tickets to place on hold.

RTS Process – Client

  1. The OC updates the project status in Notion to RTS – Client. The RTS start date is captured automatically; the OC manually lists the reason(s) for RTS in the blockers field.
  2. The OC posts in the project channel, tagging @ON Leadership, @Sales Leadership or @AM Leadership (whichever matches where the account originated), and the direct rep (Sales Rep or Account Manager), with a detailed explanation as to why the project is being returned to source:
RTS Status: Client
Client: CLIENT NAME

What the problem is: Specify: deliverables not made on time, data load not received within two weeks of kickoff, contract or payment terms misalignment, client stopping the project with no return date, or client has exhausted their allowed On-Hold extension

How to fix it: What the client needs to deliver or complete, and by when, to resolve

Contacts:
Client SME – Name – Email – Phone
Executive Sponsor – Name – Email – Phone

Relevant call recordings: Link(s) and timestamp(s) of calls where the deliverable or timeline was discussed
  1. The OC emails the client:
If missing deliverables/calls:

We haven’t received the deliverables below. All of these are critical to being able to progress the project and get you transitioned to the Rev.io Platform. Since we are not able to move forward, I will need to put your project in a “returned to source” status.

Missing deliverables:
  • deliverable 1
  • deliverable 2
  • deliverable 3
Misalignment on fees/payments missing/product expectations:
  • Due to the recent misalignment on the timing of the fees, I will need to return your account to source.
  • Since we have not been able to collect payment on your Rev.io account, I will need to return your account to source.
  • Based on our conversions about (topic misalignment), I feel it would be best to return your account to source so that we can all be on the same page on expectations.
Main Body (required, DO NOT EDIT outside of red text):

All project work — including calls, data loads, and configuration — will stop effectively immediately and will not resume until your project is back in onboarding. All importers will be removed from the development queue. Due to capacity planning, the project team will likely be reassigned. Your sales rep or account manager, REP NAME, will become your main point of contact with Rev.io during this time.

Please note that your license fee is not affected by this change. If billing has already started, it will continue on its current schedule. If it has not started yet, it will begin on MM/DD/YY as outlined in your contract, regardless of project or RTS status.

Once (insert reason for RTS) is resolved, please reach out to (SALES REP / ACCOUNT MANAGER). Our Onboarding Leadership will then assign a Rev.io project team so your weekly calls can be scheduled and a project plan with the new start and activation date can be established. This process may take up to 30 days to complete once the project is returned to Onboarding.
  1. Any calls that are scheduled will be removed from the calendar.
  2. Tickets should be placed into an on-hold status.

RTS Process – Ghosting

  1. The OC updates the project status in Notion to RTS – Ghosting. The RTS start date is captured automatically; the OC manually lists the last contact date and reason(s) for RTS in the blockers field.
  2. The OC posts in the project channel, tagging @ON Leadership, @Sales Leadership or @AM Leadership (whichever matches where the account originated), and the direct rep (Sales Rep or Account Manager), including the full outreach log:
RTS Status: Ghosting
Client: CLIENT NAME

What the problem is: Client has not responded to or shown up for communication in X weeks (2+ weeks required for this status).

Last time we heard from them: Date and channel – call, email, ticket reply, etc.

All attempts to reach them: List every attempt made, both before the last contact date and between the last contact date and this RTS notice.
  • Tickets: date(s) and ticket #
  • Emails: date(s)
  • Phone calls: date(s)
  • Other (Teams, text, etc.): date(s)
How to fix it: Outreach attempts remaining or planned – alternate contact, phone, escalation to Executive Sponsor

Contacts:
Last known Client SME – Name – Email – Phone
Executive Sponsor – Name – Email – Phone

Relevant call recordings: Link to last completed call and any missed/no-show call logs

Resuming a Project After RTS

Notification and re-engagement messaging vary by RTS status. Use the walkthrough below that matches the status the project is coming out of. Anyone tagged is expected to acknowledge within 1 business day.

Resuming – Paperwork Incomplete

  1. The direct rep (Sales Rep or Account Manager) notifies the Onboarding Consultant, tagging @ON Leadership and @Sales Leadership or @AM Leadership (whichever matches where the account originated), that the missing paperwork has been received:
Hi ________,

We have received the outstanding paperwork for CLIENT NAME. Here is a summary of our conversations and what was received.

SUMMARY OF CONVERSATIONS:
  • Summary 1
  • Summary 2
ITEMS RECEIVED:
  • Addendum
  • Contract paperwork
  • PS sign-off
Please assign an Onboarding Consultant and get the project scheduled for kickoff. Let me know if you have any questions.

Thanks,
  1. Onboarding Leadership determines who the Onboarding Consultant — and potentially the DE — will be for the project. This may change from the original project team.
  2. Once the project team is determined, the direct rep (Sales Rep or Account Manager) holds a 30-minute review meeting with the new or newly confirmed project team, covering:
    • Any reason(s) why the project went into RTS
    • What the solutions were
    • What was agreed to with the client
    • A full rundown of everything that happened since the RTS, so everyone is on the same page
  3. The direct rep (Sales Rep or Account Manager) notifies client stakeholders that the project is resuming:
Hi ________,

This email is to let you know that we have received the outstanding paperwork from your team and we are excited to get your project underway.

ITEMS RECEIVED:
  • Addendum
  • Contract paperwork
  • PS sign-off
We will now assign your onboarding team and reach out to schedule your kickoff call.

Thank you for your attention to this and please let us know if you have any questions.
